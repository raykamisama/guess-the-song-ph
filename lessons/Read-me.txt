TANGHULU KITCHEN

Online library: https://raykamisama.github.io/guess-the-song-ph/lessons/
Direct lesson: https://raykamisama.github.io/guess-the-song-ph/lessons/Tanghulu.html

Offline: keep Tanghulu.html, Library.html and the assets folder together.
Double-click Tanghulu.html and use Edge or Chrome. No installation needed.
If you downloaded the ZIP, extract it first.

1. Enter your class size and group count (up to 60 students, 8 groups,
   and 12 students per group).
2. Number the students within each group.
3. You operate the screen. The highlighted student chooses and directs.
4. Watching groups follow the prompt: mime, name fruits or answer.
5. Each group member gets a wash, dry and skewer turn.
6. You demonstrate coating and cooling, then select Next group.

Mouse/touch drag works. Large action buttons provide an easier alternative.
Sound starts off. Turn it on for short chimes. Full screen enlarges the lesson.
Progress lasts only while this tab is open. No personal information is stored.

An adult handles real sharp skewers, hot syrup and dipping.
The onscreen heating/cooling animations are demonstrations, not cooking timers.
See Teacher guide & recipe notes inside the lesson.
