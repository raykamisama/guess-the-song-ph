Teacher guide & recipe notes

Drag with a mouse or touch. Or choose a fruit, then click the large action button. This lesson uses one classroom screen: the teacher operates it while the numbered student directs each turn. Students are numbered within each group. Assign these numbers in the room. Every member gets a wash, dry and skewer turn. Watching groups answer, mime or check the step. “Next” can skip a screen for a quick demonstration; the group tracker only marks a group finished after all member turns and the coating/cooling demonstration. Select a group to return to its progress. “Replay step” resets only the current activity.

Wash hands first. An adult prepares the fruit and handles sharp skewers. The syrup and dipping are adult-only. Dry fruit completely. Use 2 parts white sugar to 1 part water by volume. An adult heats syrup to about 150°C / 300°F using a candy thermometer, dips the skewers, immediately dips the coated fruit in cold water (with ice if available) to set the sugar, then rests it on baking parchment. The onscreen timer is pretend, not a cooking timer. An adult checks cooling and suitable portions before serving. Remove cherry pits. Use peeled mandarin segments, peeled kiwi pieces and pineapple chunks, not whole unpeeled fruits.
